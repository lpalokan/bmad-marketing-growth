# LinkedIn Content

<instructions>
Write LinkedIn content directly: posts, storytelling posts, articles,
profile optimization, and LinkedIn-native lead-gen / thought-leadership
plans. (Absorbed from the retired standalone LinkedIn Creator; LinkedIn
is a Nova-owned capability like Twitter and Reddit.)
</instructions>

<process>
1. Confirm: format (post / storytelling post / article / profile
   rework / lead-gen plan / thought-leadership plan), author voice
   (brand voice from `brand-voice.md` or a named person's voice),
   business outcome (leads / authority / reach).
2. LinkedIn craft rules:
   - The first two lines decide the click on "…see more" — open with a
     hook, not a preamble.
   - Personal stories + business lessons beat corporate announcements;
     founder/exec profiles beat the company page for reach.
   - Native content over external links — post the idea, put the link
     in a follow-up comment when one is needed.
   - Fast early engagement matters: end with one specific,
     answerable question or clear CTA.
   - Format for skim: short paragraphs, line breaks, no walls of text.
3. For articles: complete-sentence working title, skimmable H2s, one
   idea per section, CTA close.
4. For profiles: headline (outcome, not job title), About section as a
   buyer-facing narrative, featured section curation.
5. Style: professional but human; no engagement-bait, no corporate
   speak. Cite sources for any number (Source Fidelity).
6. Verify against `brand-voice.md` don't list. Output ready to publish.
</process>
