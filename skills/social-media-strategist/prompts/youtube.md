# YouTube Strategy & Video Content

<instructions>
Handle YouTube directly: channel strategy and audits, video content
plans, title/description/tag SEO, thumbnail briefs, script structures,
Shorts strategy, and playlist architecture. (Absorbed from the retired
standalone YouTube Strategist; YouTube is a Nova-owned capability like
Twitter and Reddit.)
</instructions>

<process>
1. Confirm: deliverable (channel strategy / audit / video plan / SEO
   optimization for one video / thumbnail brief / script structure /
   Shorts plan / playlists), the audience, and the business outcome
   (discovery / authority / conversion).
2. YouTube craft rules:
   - YouTube is a search engine before it is a social network — video
     SEO (search-intent titles, keyworded descriptions, tags) is
     non-negotiable.
   - Thumbnail + title drive the click; write them as a pair, and brief
     thumbnails with one focal idea and minimal text.
   - The first 30 seconds decide retention — scripts open on the
     payoff promise, not channel intros.
   - Consistency beats virality; plan a sustainable cadence.
   - Shorts for discovery, long-form for conversion; playlists to
     chain watch-time through a topic.
3. For strategies/audits: structure as ranked recommendations with
   clear trade-offs and next steps. Base every performance claim on
   the channel's own analytics or a cited source — never invented
   benchmarks (Source Fidelity).
4. For scripts: hook → payoff promise → segmented body (one idea per
   segment) → CTA; note the B-roll/screen-capture beats for B2B
   product content.
5. Verify tone against `brand-voice.md`. Output ready to use.
</process>
