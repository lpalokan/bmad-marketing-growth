# Marketing ↔ Sales Handoff Motion

<instructions>
Design one concrete handoff between the wings so an account narrative,
signal, or asset crosses the boundary without losing fidelity. Output is
a short handoff spec both sides follow — Rae designs it; the wings
execute it through the standard brief-driven protocol.
</instructions>

<process>
1. Identify the handoff shape (ask if unclear). The recurring ones:
   - **ABM ↔ prospecting** — Frank Field / Aldo ABM warm named accounts;
     Sam Sell's motion works the warmed tier. Shared artifact: the tiered
     account list + per-account storyline.
   - **Launch → outbound** — Priya Position / Lana Launch produce the
     launch narrative and sales bundle; Stella Story adapts it into
     account storylines; Aria Approach and Casey Cadence carry it into
     sequences.
   - **Signals → campaigns** — Sage Signal's triggers feed Dani Demand
     (retargeting, demand-gen audiences) or Frank Field (event/ABM
     plays).
   - **Content → touches** — Milo Page's pieces become Casey Cadence's
     value-add touches and Aria Approach's openers.
   - **Won/active accounts → advocacy** — Ricky Reply's booked meetings
     and closed accounts feed Cara Customer's advocate pipeline.
2. For the chosen handoff, specify:
   - **Source** (wing, agent, artifact + path under `{output_folder}`)
     and **consumer** (wing, agent, what they do with it).
   - **Trigger** — what event makes the handoff fire (list frozen, launch
     date, signal detected).
   - **Fidelity rule** — what must survive the crossing unchanged (the
     value hypothesis, the tier, the source citations).
   - **Brief** — which orchestrator issues the consuming brief and which
     template they use (Sam's `brief-templates/` or the domain
     orchestrator's).
3. Confirm both sides' orchestrators are named and no specialist is
   briefed directly by Rae.
4. Write the spec to
   `{output_folder}/work/{handoff-id}/handoff-spec.md` and tell the user
   which orchestrator to activate first.
</process>
