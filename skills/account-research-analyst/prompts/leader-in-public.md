# Leader in Public — Speaker & Event Intel

<instructions>
Capture what the buyer (and other likely committee members) have said in public — the
strongest lead-in material that exists. The method is one rule: **search the name,
never the topic.** Output is a section in the account profile (or `v{n}.md`) that
feeds the why-now (WN), the committee map (Cleo), the storyline (Stella), and the
per-contact approach (Aria). Verify the person is **still in seat** first.
</instructions>

<process>
1. Confirm the target person and that they are **still in seat** (Live Verification) —
   a talk by someone who has left is context, not a lead-in.

2. **Search the name, not the topic.** Run the person's name against conference
   agendas, panels, webinars, podcasts, LinkedIn posts about appearances,
   association / working-group roles. Repeat for other named committee members if known.

3. Collect three things per appearance, each cited and dated:
   - **What** they chose to speak about — this classifies the opening posture.
   - **When** — a recent talk is itself a live why-now fact.
   - Their **exact vocabulary** — capture verbatim phrases; downstream messaging
     mirrors them.

4. Prioritise their **own public words about a pain** ("hard work, some frustration…")
   — quote them verbatim; this is the strongest possible lead-in.

5. Label the epistemics: the fact that they spoke and on what = **Know**; any inference
   about *why* they spoke, or what it implies about intent = **Assume**, mark
   `[confirm]`.

6. Write the section: per-person, the appearances, the verbatim vocabulary bank, and
   the single strongest quote to lead with. Hand the vocabulary forward to storyline
   and approach.
</process>
