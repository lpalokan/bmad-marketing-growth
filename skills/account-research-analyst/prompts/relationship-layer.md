# Capture the Relationship Layer (Warm / Active Accounts Only)

<instructions>
For a **warm or active** account only, capture what the relationship holder knows that
the open web does not — the strongest unlocks usually live here. Output is a **clearly
separated** section in the account profile (or `v{n}.md`), never blended with public
facts. If the account is cold (no existing relationship), skip this capability and say
so. Label every unsupported estimate `[Account note — needs verification]`.
</instructions>

<process>
1. Confirm the account is warm/active and identify **who holds the relationship**
   (AE, partner, exec sponsor). If nobody does, this is a cold account — stop and note
   it; public research (§1–§4) stands alone.

2. Ask the relationship holder the six questions, and record each answer as an
   **Account note**:
   1. Who asked, and from which function?
   2. What POC or workshop has already happened, and what did it prove?
   3. What data sources, evidence types, policies, or systems were used?
   4. What did the customer like, reject, or ask to explore next?
   5. What internal decision, outsourcing option, or operating model is now being
      challenged?
   6. What scale facts are known — people, regions, controls, systems, evidence
      variants, volumes, cadence?

3. Always ask specifically for **partner/internal material** the open web won't have
   (an email thread, a workshop output, a client-story URL, a POC result) — read it
   carefully; it is often the pivot.

4. Keep this layer **separate** from the public sections. Label unsupported estimates
   `[Account note — needs verification]`. For an **active POC**, POC facts lead and
   public research is context; make that ordering explicit in the section.

5. Do not let an Account note silently become a public **Know** — it is confirmed
   about *this relationship*, but still verify anything time-sensitive (Live
   Verification).

6. Write the separated section and flag which notes most change the framing.
</process>
