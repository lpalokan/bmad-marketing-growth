# Research method

How to build the record for one account. Applies to
`account-research-analyst` and to anyone verifying a fact before publication.

## Read our own context first

Before the first fetch, read the company-context bundle per the skill's
activation step — the map in `index.md`, the core, the sales layer, and the
layers under the hubs (`offerings/`, `personas/`, `playbooks/`, `case-studies/`),
plus any nested sub-bundle.

This is not background reading. It decides what counts as a signal. An account
fact is only load-bearing because some offering, persona or trigger in our
context makes it so, and a researcher who has not read the offering components
cannot tell a why-now from a news item. Research done before the context is read
gets redone.

Note which concepts were present and which were absent. A missing playbook or
persona set changes how grounded the dossier can be.

## Fetch order

Work outward from what the company says about itself to what others say about it.

1. **The company's own site.** Who they are, structure, ownership, what they sell.
2. **The latest results release.** Half-year or annual, whichever is newer. This
   is the source for every financial figure. Prefer the release over a summary.
3. **The prior period's release.** Comparatives are what make a number an
   argument.
4. **The governance page.** Structure, control model, lines of defence, any legal
   duty the entity carries over others.
5. **The leadership page.** Verified live, today. This is the only acceptable
   source for a reader-facing committee table.
6. **The regulator or supervisory register**, where the industry has one.
   Findings, penalties, live processes, and their exact dates.
7. **The news feed.** Their own releases first, in the original language.
   Acquisitions, appointments, programme milestones, dated.
8. **Enrichment**, per `enrichment-and-provenance.md`, to widen the people layer.
9. **Sector or peer material**, only where it is genuinely load-bearing, and read
   `third-parties.md` before any of it enters the dossier.

## Read the primary document, not a summary

A search result summary is not a source. When a results release is a PDF, fetch
it and read the pages. Search engines regularly attribute one company's figures
to another with a similar name, and the summary will look confident either way.

If two companies in the same market have similar names, verify which entity every
figure belongs to before recording it.

## What must be verified live in this session

- Every named person, with exact title and whether still in seat.
- Every penalty, enforcement action or supervisory process, with its date.
- Any published price, offer or commercial term.
- Any figure that will appear in the executive summary.

## Finding the why-now

A why-now is a dated event that changes what the account has to do. Look for:

- A transaction. Acquisition, disposal, merger, offer period with a close date.
- A programme with a go-live window.
- A supervisory or regulatory event, including one still open.
- A leadership appointment into the buying seat.
- A ratio that has moved for more than one period.
- A published target with a deadline.

Record each with its date, its domain, and a strength read. Weak signals stay in
with their weakness stated. An honest medium beats an inflated high.

## Naming the gaps

Name what you do not know precisely enough that a person in a meeting could
answer it. "Tooling unknown" is not a gap. "What tooling supports control testing
and workpapers today, or does the team work in documents and spreadsheets" is.

Name the single most decision-relevant gap explicitly. It is usually the meeting
objective.

## Exit check before handing off

- The company-context bundle was read before research began, including the
  layers under the hubs, and what was absent is noted.
- Every load-bearing fact is KNOW, ASSUME or GAP, with links on the KNOWs.
- Leaders, penalties and prices were verified live this session.
- No peer or case-study detail has been attributed to this account.
- At least one confirmed pain candidate exists.
- The top gap is named as a meeting objective.
- Every figure has been traced to the entity it actually belongs to.
